EvoAI is a C++17 library of building blocks to evolve, train and cluster **small** models, neural networks
that are evolved (NEAT, HyperNEAT) and / or trained (backpropagation), self-organizing maps, and the algorithms around them.

Features
--------

- A :class:`Genome<EvoAI::Genome>` to use NEAT and HyperNEAT algorithms to evolve :class:`Neural Networks<EvoAI::NeuralNetwork>` .
- A Population(with speciation) to evolve your own :class:`Genomes<EvoAI::Genome>` , it only needs to fulfill :var:`meta::is_populable_v\<MyClass\><EvoAI::meta::is_populable_v>`
- Common :any:`Selection Algorithms<EvoAI::SelectionAlgorithms>`.
- Train neural networks using backpropagation, enabling them to learn from labeled datasets, with the optimizers :class:`SGD<EvoAI::SGD>`, :class:`Adam<EvoAI::Adam>` and :class:`Muon<EvoAI::Muon>`, learning rate schedulers and loss functions.
- A ``SelfOrganizingMap`` (Kohonen) to cluster feature vectors with any distance metric: best matching units with their distance, evaluation (quantization and topographic error, how many samples each node won), a U-matrix, training that can be resumed and stopped, and JSON saving and loading.
- Evaluators to measure a network (neurons, connections, parameters, memory, sparsity, latency) and to combine the measures into an objective.
- A generic ``Graph`` and ``LatticeGraph`` with pathfinding algorithms and maze generation.
